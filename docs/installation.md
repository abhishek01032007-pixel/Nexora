## ðŸ—‘ï¸ Uninstallation

To uninstall Nexora Skills Manager:
1. Open **Windows Settings** -> **Apps** -> **Installed Apps**.
2. Locate **Nexora Skills Manager**.
3. Click **Uninstall** and follow the prompts.

*Your personal project workspaces and custom skill files are strictly preserved during uninstallation.*
